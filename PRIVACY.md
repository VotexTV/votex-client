# Votex Client Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Votex Client is maintained by an individual. This policy describes the Windows launcher source currently published in this repository. Contact the maintainer through the repository's GitHub Issues page for privacy questions.

## Data handled on your device

The launcher stores Minecraft profiles, launcher settings, downloaded game files, and account-session data needed for features you choose. This information is stored locally in the application data and Minecraft directories configured by you.

## Network requests

When you use the launcher, it connects to services needed for the feature you request. These may include Microsoft and Xbox authentication, Mojang/Minecraft metadata and download services, Fabric and other mod-loader services, Modrinth or CurseForge when browsing or downloading mods, and the Votex project website. Those services receive the network information required to handle the request, such as your IP address and requested resource. Their own privacy policies apply.

The launcher does not send profile files, worlds, or local settings to the Votex maintainer as telemetry. An account sign-in sends authentication data directly to the selected identity provider. Do not share your account credentials with the Votex maintainer.

## Retention and choices

Local profiles, settings, and cached downloads remain on your computer until you remove them. You can sign out or delete local launcher data through Windows and the launcher controls. Removing local data may remove profiles and cached game files.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the launcher’s data handling changes. The current version is published in the Votex Client source repository.
